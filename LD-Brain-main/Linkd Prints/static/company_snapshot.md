# Linkd Prints — Company Snapshot
**Founded: 2021 | Location: Bhiwandi factory**
**Role in Group: Growth engine + LD Silk's captive printer. #1 cashflow lever for the self-funded 1,000 Cr journey.**

---

## What It Is

Digital sublimation printing on poly-based substrates. Bhiwandi factory. Runs on three distinct books simultaneously.

---

- **Profitable**: Yes — on Book B only
- **Printing machines (total)**: 10 | **Active**: 5 | **Idle**: 5
- **Fusing machines (total)**: 2 | Both active
- **Active printing capacity**: 17,800 mtrs/day | ~534,000 mtrs/month theoretical

- **Spare practical capacity**: **~185,451 mtrs/month**
- **External registered customers (Book B T12)**: 28 verified active parties
- **Book B repeat rate**: 80%

## Competitive Environment

Price-war market. Many small digital printers in Mumbai/Bhiwandi with 1–2 machines undercutting on price. Low barriers to entry for small operators.

**Linkd's position**:
- Cannot win on price — 12-machine fixed-cost base means lean small operators will always quote cheaper
- Must win on: quality + TAT + vertical story + reliability at scale

## Brain Rules (Quick Reference)

1. **Three books, three playbooks.** Book A (captive break-even) / Book B1 (traders, thin) / Book B2 (brands, target). Never conflate.
2. **Book A is ballast, not profit.** Every external mtr is disproportionately margin-accretive.
3. **Book B2 is built from zero.** The NBD hire's job is to open a direct-brand channel that doesn't exist today.
4. **Price wars are for losers.** Don't fight small printers on price. Win on quality, vertical story, reliability.
5. **Nandu Bhai disruption cascades into two companies.** Retention + indirect SOP capture is non-negotiable.
6. **728 Silk customers have never used Linkd.** Filter for broker return rate before routing — high-return Silk brokers (>30%) must not be activated for Linkd until their Silk behavior is restructured.
7. **Jobwork rate with LD Silk must be documented and questioned.** Break-even may be a structural Silk subsidy. Dilip-owned.
8. **Two ERPs = reconciliation discipline.** SAB (revenue ₹) and custom (production mtrs) must agree. Mahesh owns the bridge.
9. **Sublimation = polyester only.** Never promise sublimation on natural fibers.
10. **Korean paper is the quality moat.** Never compromise on paper quality.
11. **The rate card is the floor — for printing.** 58"/62" = ₹28 (with calender). 72" = ₹45. Fusing-only jobs are a separate service.
12. **5 idle machines = zero-capex capacity reserve.** Don't reactivate prematurely. But when Book B2 hits 250K+ mtrs/month, idle machines are the first expansion lever.
13. **Inter-company rates must be consistent.** Silk at break-even (possible subsidy) and Cotton at ₹35–40 (above market) are contradictions. One policy, both directions. Dilip owns.
