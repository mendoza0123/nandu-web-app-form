# Linkd Prints — The Three-Book Model
**Last Updated: 2026-04-20**

> Linkd is NOT a single-sided business. It runs three distinct books on the same machines. Never conflate them — each has different economics, different playbooks, different strategic value.

---

## Book A — LD Silk Captive Jobwork (~126,865 mtrs/month)

- LD Silk sells digitally printed fabric. **All of it is printed at Linkd** on a jobwork arrangement.
- **Economics: BREAK-EVEN for Linkd.** Pays the bills and absorbs fixed cost. Does NOT generate meaningful margin.
- Book A is **operational ballast, not a profit center.**
- Volume grows only if LD Silk's end-customer demand for printed fabric grows → This is a **Silk sales problem**, not a Linkd problem.
- **Verified avg implied rate**: ₹36.75/mtr (₹5.60 Cr ÷ 15,22,383 mtrs — Linkd's own jobwork ERP)
- **Question on the table**: Is break-even the right rate? Is Linkd subsidizing Silk? Dilip-owned analysis.

*Book A volume verified from TWO sources:*
- *SAB customer master (April 2026): CUST2518 L.D.SILK MILLS (JOB) = 1,459,216 mtrs T12 = 122K mtrs/month*
- *Linkd jobwork ERP extract (April 2026): LD Silk (JOB) = 1,522,382.73 mtrs = **126,865 mtrs/month** ← use this as primary*

---

## Book B1 — External Traders (~36,597 mtrs/month)

- Current external book is **mostly traders** — middlemen who get fabric printed to sell downstream.
- **Economics**: Thin margin, transactional relationship, easy to switch printers, price-sensitive.
- Verified 28 distinct parties in T12 (from Linkd jobwork ERP extract).
- 80% repeat rate = strong signal that the product quality retains even trader-grade customers.
- Revenue flat for months despite headroom — traders don't scale volume, they arbitrage margins.
- **Avg Book B jobwork rate**: ₹45.97/mtr — higher than Book A (₹36.75/mtr), confirming external work carries better margin.
- **Strategy**: Maintain as baseline volume. Do NOT invest to grow this layer. Apply price floors; let unprofitable traders go to competitors.

---

## Book B2 — Direct Brands (0 TODAY — THE GROWTH TARGET)

- Linkd has **zero direct-brand relationships today.**
- Target customers: apparel brands, D2C labels, fashion houses, uniform suppliers.
- **Economics**: Higher margin, stickier relationships, design-led conversations, larger wallet share per account.
- **NBD hire's job**: Open this channel from zero using a consultative selling approach.
- The 36K → 150K external mtrs/month plan = **primarily a Book B2 build**.

## What the Book Split Means for Capacity

- Book A at ~126,865 verified (may grow if Silk's printed-fabric sales grow); LD Cotton at Linkd = 1,087/month (small, stable)
- Book B1 stays at ~35–40K (maintain, don't invest)
- **Book B2 grows from 0 to 100–150K** via NBD + Silk cross-sell + direct-brand outbound
- Adding 100–150K mtrs/month at 20–35% Book B2 margin = **multi-crore annual contribution lift with zero new capex**
- Beyond 300K: reactivate idle 3-head machines — no new machine investment until ~440K+ mtrs/month
