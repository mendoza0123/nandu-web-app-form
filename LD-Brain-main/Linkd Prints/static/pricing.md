| **72"** | ₹44 | ₹1 | ₹1 | ₹45 | ₹45 | ₹46 |

**Notes:**
- 58"/62"/64" are the same base rate — treated as one width band
- 72" commands a significant premium (₹44 vs ₹27) — wide-width is a specialty service
- Calender and Rolling are ₹1 each — convenience services, not margin drivers
- **This rate card IS the price floor for printing jobs.** No printing job below these rates without explicit Raghav sign-off.
- **Fusing-only jobs are a separate service** (e.g. KAIVALYA DIGIHOUSE at ₹9/58"). Separate fusing rate card needed.

## ⚠️ LD Cotton Mills — Inter-Company Rate Problem

LD Cotton Mills is billed at ₹35–40 for 62" fabric — **25–43% above standard market rate of ₹28.**

- **Silk → Linkd**: Linkd charges break-even (possibly subsidizing Silk)
- **Linkd → Cotton**: Linkd charges Cotton above market rate (Cotton is overpaying)

**Impact on Cotton**: ₹7–12 extra per mtr in processing cost. On a garment using 1.5–2 mtrs, that's ₹10–24 per garment of avoidable cost. On 30,000 garments/month = ₹3–7 lakh/month above-market cost baked into Cotton's P&L.

**Action (Dilip + Raghav)**: Inter-company rate review must cover BOTH directions. The group needs one consistent inter-company pricing policy.
