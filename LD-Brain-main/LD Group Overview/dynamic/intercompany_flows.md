## Inter-Company Pricing Conflicts ⚠️

### Silk → Linkd (Book A) — Possible Subsidy

- Linkd charges Silk ₹36.75/mtr implied average
- Break-even rate for Linkd — no margin generated
- Question: Is Linkd subsidizing Silk's printed fabric business?
- **Owner of resolution**: Dilip + Raghav
- **Status**: OPEN — written jobwork rate policy not documented

### Linkd → Cotton — Above-Market Rate

- Linkd charges Cotton ₹35–40/mtr for 62" printing
- Market rate for 62": ₹28/mtr
- Cotton is **overpaying ₹7–12/mtr** — adds ₹10–24 per garment of avoidable cost
- On 30,000 garments/month = ₹3–7 lakh/month in above-market cost
- **This partially explains why Cotton's margins are below floor**
- **Owner of resolution**: Dilip + Raghav
- **Status**: OPEN — group-level inter-company pricing policy does not exist

### Required Action

A single consistent inter-company pricing policy must cover BOTH directions:
- Silk → Linkd (may be too low / subsidizing Silk)
- Linkd → Cotton (currently too high / penalizing Cotton)
- **Dilip owns** the analysis and policy document.
