## Why Mills Are Tier-0 Assets

1. **Range depends on supply**: Breadth of 1,648 SKUs exists because mills trust LD enough to share new developments early.
2. **Pricing depends on trust**: Long-tenure mills give first-look pricing and absorb rupee volatility on favor terms.
3. **Credit depends on payment history**: 35 years of on-time payments = a relationship moat impossible to replicate.
4. **Opportunistic buys depend on access**: Best distress sales come to the 5 buyers the mill calls first.

---

## Mill Brain Rules

1. **Pay on time. Always.** Late payment = fastest way to lose cost advantage and range access.
2. **Visit, don't just call.** One in-person visit per year to top-5 mills.
3. **Remember the family.** Festival greetings, weddings, condolences. Memory is currency.
4. **Never play mills against each other.** Loyalty builds cost advantage; shopping kills it.
5. **Early warning**: Mill goes quiet → founder touch immediately.
