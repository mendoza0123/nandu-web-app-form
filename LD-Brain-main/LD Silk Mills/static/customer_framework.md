## Revenue Concentration Reality

**Prior assumption (wrong): Top 10 = 40%+ of revenue.**
**Verified reality (Phase 1): Top 10 = 14.74%.**

LD Silk is a **long-tail distribution business** — revenue is spread across hundreds of small-to-mid customers, not concentrated in a handful of whales. This is actually a resilience advantage. No single customer loss is catastrophic.

**The real risk is not concentration — it is the long-tail churn problem.**

---

## Retention Problem (Structural Analysis)

**Scale of the problem:**
- 57.1% churn rate — more than half of every customer ever acquired placed zero orders in FY 25-26
- 31.1% one-and-done — nearly one in three customers across three years bought exactly once and never returned
- Acquisition spend on one-and-done customers is entirely wasted

**Root causes:**
1. No ICP defined — acquires whoever shows up → high one-and-done rates from misfit buyers
2. No reactivation system — no cadence, no outreach, no win-back campaign for 976 dormants
3. SP incentives reward new orders, not retention
4. No early-warning system — a customer going quiet is not visible until fully churned

**Reactivation math (conservative):**
- 976 dormant accounts at ₹0 acquisition cost
- 10% reactivation → 97 returning customers
- Avg returning customer = ₹2–3L/year → **₹1.9–2.9 Cr at zero new CAC**
- 20% reactivation → **₹4–6 Cr incremental**

## ICP — To Be Defined

ICP dimensions to capture:
- **Customer type**: Retailer / wholesaler / manufacturer / export house / designer / D2C brand
- **Order size band**: Sweet spot for margin × frequency × retention?
- **Geography**: Which cities repeat best?
- **Product mix**: Which fabric categories correlate with multi-year retention?
- **Price sensitivity**: Which price tier customers stay longest?
- **Cross-sell readiness**: Who buys print-friendly poly fabrics? (= warm Linkd leads)

## Cross-Sell to Linkd Framework

**Target**: Filter Silk customers buying Digital Quality / polyester substrates (Section 5.10 Tier 4) → these are the warmest Linkd leads by product logic.

**Playbook (priority-ordered):**
1. **Amarson case study**: Build a 1-page deck showing Amarson's journey from Silk → Silk + Linkd. Use as outreach anchor.
2. Filter Silk customers buying Digital Quality / poly substrates → verified warmest Linkd leads
3. Offer free sample printing on their existing fabric — zero risk for the customer
4. Target: 10% conversion of top 500 Silk customers = 50 new Linkd accounts within 12 months
5. Track cross-sell conversion per SP → reward it explicitly in SP comp structure

## Return-Rate Thresholds

- **Maximum Return Threshold (MRT)**: >35% trailing-12-month return rate triggers mandatory account review
- **Review outcome options**: Structural conversation / Order gate / De-prioritize / Exit
- **Broker threshold**: >30% return rate triggers mandatory structural conversation
