## Core Principle: The Resale Thesis Rule

Before any opportunistic purchase, write 3 lines:
1. **Who** buys this (named customer or segment)
2. **What price** (floor, target, stretch)
3. **By when** (specific month or season)

**No thesis = no buy.** Exception: strategic relationship maintenance with top-5 mill, with explicit sign-off.

## Dead Stock Audit (Active Project)

**Status**: Active | **Priority**: Housekeeping (under ₹1 Cr locked)

**Two goals**:
1. **Liquidate** — turn dead fabric into cash, stop paying storage costs
2. **Learn** — every dead SKU is a buying mistake. Extract the heuristic before liquidating.

**Classification buckets**:

| Bucket | Definition | Recovery % | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Good quality, out-of-trend | 60–80% | Seasonal rotation or value buyers |
| B | Good quality, wrong customer match | 40–70% | Find right channel |
| C | Quality drift (aged, yellowing) | 20–40% | Urgent price-to-move |
| D | Genuine overstock of a winner | 80–100% | Velocity incentives through normal channels |
| E | Strategic holds (intentional) | n/a | Document why; don't liquidate |

**Liquidation channels (priority order)**:
1. Top Silk customers — first right of refusal
2. Liquidation wholesalers — cash-and-carry
3. Export trading networks
4. **Linkd integration** — dead poly Silk fabrics → Linkd print blanks
5. **Vhagar integration** — dead stock in Vhagar drops where composition fits
6. Charity / craft sector — last resort

**Rule**: No SKU gets liquidated without a 1-paragraph post-mortem written into this file.
