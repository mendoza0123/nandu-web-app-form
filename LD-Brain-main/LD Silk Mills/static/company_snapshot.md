# LD Silk Mills — Company Snapshot
**Founded: 1991 | Location: Kalbadevi, Mumbai (office) + Bhiwandi (warehouse/ops)**
**Role in Group: Cash cow + foundation. Funds Cotton + Vhagar. Largest Linkd customer.**

---

## What It Is

Trading house for woven fabrics (silk, poly, blends) + large volumes of digitally printed fabric.
**ALL printing is executed at Linkd Prints** (Bhiwandi) on a jobwork model — LD Silk does not own printing machinery.

---

## Scale

- **Revenue**: 50–80 Cr (estimate — bulk of group's 90–100 Cr)
- **Gross Margin**: 12–18%
- **Profitable**: Yes. Cash engine of the group.
- **Customer master (SAB total codes)**: 5,974 registered codes
- **3-year ordering base**: 1,708 unique customers (placed ≥1 order in last 3 years)
- **Trailing-12-month active**: ~732 customers (FY 25-26)
- **SAB stock codes**: 1,648 | **Distinct quality names**: 620
- **Actively selling (T12)**: 275 quality names (159 healthy + 116 stocked out)
- **Dead/Dormant (stock, no sales)**: 65 | **Obsolete**: 280
- **Salespeople**: ~20 (3–5 drive 70%+ of revenue)
- **Agents**: 576 registered | 220 active (verified Phase 2)

## Moat Stack (Confirmed, in Order)

1. **Range breadth** — 1,648 SKU master. Customers come because they find what they need only here.
2. **Reliability** — Quality consistency + on-time delivery + fewer surprises than competitors.
3. **Relationships** — 35 years of trust with mills + agents + top customers.
4. **Vertical integration** — Linkd prints + Cotton garments under one group (underused story).

---

## Competitive Landscape

Fragmented — many small players, no dominant rival. LD Silk's range moat is defensible because nobody else has consolidated that breadth in the same category.

---

## Brain Rules (Quick Reference)

1. **No anonymous top anchors.** Every key account has a name, relationship owner, and last-touch date.
2. **Proactive beats reactive.** If a top customer only hears from us during a problem, we're already losing.
3. **31.1% one-and-done is a structural failure, not bad luck.** 531 customers bought once and never came back. Reactivating 976 dormant is higher-leverage than any new acquisition spend.
4. **Cross-sell is free leverage.** Silk → Linkd is the first test. Measure SP-level conversion.
5. **Gross revenue is a vanity metric for any channel with variable returns.** Net Broker Value is the only reliable sort for agents.
6. **>30% return rate = structural conversation, not silent tolerance.**
7. **Resale thesis or no buy.** The 3-line rule. Non-negotiable going forward.
8. **Dead stock is live cash.** Treat the audit as an aggressive collection project.
9. **Range moat = deliberate breadth.** Some "dead" SKUs protect the moat — don't liquidate blindly.
