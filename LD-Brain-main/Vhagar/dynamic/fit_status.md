# Vhagar — Fit Status & PMF Sprint
**Last Updated: 2026-04-20**

---

## Current Fit Status

**Status: NOT LOCKED.** Fit varies across sizes AND across drops. Same "M" from two drops can fit differently.

**Return rate**: Under 10% (better than feared for D2C fashion at 799–1799 in India)

---

## Reframe: Not an Emergency — An Unlock

At <10% returns, fit inconsistency is **NOT an active emergency**. Customers tolerate some variance at 799–1799.

**BUT**: At 2,500+ (the aspiration), customers expect locked fit. Inconsistency kills repeat purchase and justifies returns.

**Fit-lock sprint is what enables the pricing journey from 799–1799 → 2,500+.**

---

## The Fit-Lock Sprint (2–3 Weeks — Not Yet Started)

1. **Master size chart** — lock exact measurements for S/M/L/XL/XXL for shirts (core product first)
2. **Fit blocks** — physical reference garments per size, stored at Cotton factory
3. **Pre-drop QC** — every new drop measured against the block before any unit ships
4. **Tolerance bands** — written, numeric, enforced (e.g., ±0.5cm on chest, ±0.3cm on collar)
5. **Return analysis** — extract existing returns, cluster by size + product, identify worst drifts

**Owner**: Raghav + Naushi jointly | **Venue**: Cotton factory | **Timeline**: 2–3 weeks from decision
