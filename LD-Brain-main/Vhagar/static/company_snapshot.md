# Vhagar — Company Snapshot
**Founded: January 2026 | Website: vhagar.co**
**Role in Group: D2C brand moonshot. Premium consumer face of the group. Captive anchor for Cotton. Vertical integration story made visible.**

---

## What It Is

D2C **menswear shirts** brand. Core product: shirts at **799–1799 INR MRP**. Continuous core catalog + periodic themed drops. Aspiring premium — future pricing moves **upward** (toward 2,500+), never downward.

---

## Scale (Infancy Stage)

- **Revenue**: <1 Cr (infancy)
- **Drops completed**: 1–3 (very early — every drop is a learning exercise)
- **Budget envelope**: **₹75 lakhs – 1.5 Cr / year** (committed) across inventory, marketing, team, tools
- **Stage**: Launch mode

---

## Competitive Set

- **NOT**: Bewakoof / Souled Store (lower price tier)
- **Current peers**: Snitch / Turms / House of Rare
- **Aspiration**: Rare Rabbit tier (premium menswear, 2,500+)

---

## Founder Visibility

**NONE.** Raghav stays behind the scenes. Brand must stand entirely on its own.

---

## Brain Rules (Quick Reference)

1. **Two-principal ownership.** Raghav leads execution; Naushi leads design + co-decides.
2. **Fit-lock is the premium migration unlock.** Not an emergency at <10% returns, but blocks the 2,500+ pricing ambition.
3. **The vertical story is the single biggest untapped brand asset.** Deploy only after fit is locked.
4. **Founder stays off camera.** No Raghav face, no Raghav voice in any brand surface.
5. **Every drop is a learning exercise at 1–3 drops.** Post-drop review within 48 hours — always.
6. **Measure repeat purchase like oxygen.** It's the only number that tells the truth in D2C apparel.
7. **The brain disagrees on marketing scale before fit-lock — and tracks the disagreement.** Founder override honored. Signals watched.
8. **Vhagar is Cotton's anchor customer.** Protecting Vhagar protects Cotton's utilization and the group's vertical story.
9. **Gate 1 (100 real customers) before any scale talk.**
