# LD Cotton Mills — Certification Roadmap
**Last Updated: 2026-04-20**

---

## Cert Sprint (Dilip-Owned — Critical Path)

This is the unlock for everything else. Without certs, there are no mid-tier buyers, no export buyers, and no credible Cotton sales story.

**Target**: ≥2 of 4 certs live within 90 days. Without this, Steps 2–5 of capacity fill playbook are blocked.

---

## Certification Plan

| Cert | Purpose | Priority | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ISO 9001** | Quality management system | **#1** | 60–90 days from kickoff |
| **OEKO-TEX** | Textile chemical safety | **#2** | 60–90 days |
| **SEDEX (SMETA)** | Ethical sourcing | #3 | 90–120 days |
| **BSCI** | Business social compliance | #4 | 90–120 days |

---

## What Each Cert Unlocks

```
ISO 9001 ─────────────────────► Mid-tier domestic + general credibility
    └── + OEKO-TEX ────────────► Branded apparel + export-lite
           └── + SEDEX (SMETA) ► EU/US mid-market buyers
                  └── + BSCI ──► Major EU retailers
```

## Kill Switch Gates (Tied to Cert Progress)

| Gate | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Month 6 | Certs moving + 2+ mid-tier sample orders | Continue |
| Month 6 | Certs moving + no mid-tier traction yet | Accelerate outreach; review sales approach |
| Month 6 | Certs stalled + no mid-tier traction | 🔴 Escalate to family council |
| Month 12 | ≥60K/month at margins above floor | Accelerate — target 80%+ by month 18 |
| Month 12 | 45–55K/month with improving margin mix | Continue with tighter discipline |
| Month 12 | Still at 30–35K/month or margin-negative | Structural decision required |
| Month 18 | — | Hard gate. No further extensions without specific joint family plan. |

### Structural Decision Options (if targets missed)

1. **Mothball excess capacity** — reduce to Vhagar-captive micro-factory
2. **Reframe as Vhagar-captive only** — smaller, profitable, focused
3. **Sell / lease unused lines** — recover capex
4. **Full wind-down** — exit garmenting

*The kill switch is not a threat. It is a promise to the family that Cotton will not drift indefinitely on compromise-culture inertia.*
