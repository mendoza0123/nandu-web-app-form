## Three Tiers at Cotton

### MASS
- Customer: domestic mass retailers, white-label for value brands
- Price point: Lowest
- Volume: High-volume, repeat of standard SKUs
- DHU target: <40–50
- Compliance: Basic — certs not required
- Fabric: Commodity cotton, poly-cotton blends
- Lead time: 30–45 days from PO
- **Status**: LAST RESORT — decline unless margin floor is met

### MID (Target Tier for Fill)
- Customer: domestic branded apparel, export-lite buyers
- Price point: 1.5–2× mass
- Volume: Medium runs with customization
- DHU target: <20–30
- Compliance: ISO 9001 preferred, OEKO-TEX useful
- Fabric: Higher-count cottons, combed yarns
- Lead time: 45–60 days from PO

### EXPORT
- Customer: international buying houses, global brands, EU/US retailers
- Price point: 2–4× mass
- Volume: Style-specific, season-driven, tight windows
- DHU target: <5–15
- Compliance: **All 4 certs required** — ISO 9001, OEKO-TEX, SEDEX, BSCI
- Fabric: Certified, traceable, often buyer-specified
- Lead time: 60–90 days with milestone reviews

## Quality Standards

### DHU — Defects Per Hundred Units

| Tier | Target DHU | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Mass | <40–50 | Acceptable for price-sensitive domestic |
| Mid | <20–30 | Branded domestic, some export |
| Export | <5–15 | International buyer requirements |
| Premium | <3 | High-price goods only |

### Garment Defect Categories

**Critical defects (REJECT):**
- Holes in visible areas
- Open seams in load-bearing joints
- Missing operations (buttons, labels, zippers)
- Wrong size or wrong shade

**Major defects (Rework or Reject):**
- Open seams in non-critical areas; broken stitches beyond tolerance
- Stains on visible areas; measurements out of tolerance
- Puckering; wrong thread / shade mismatch; poor buttonhole / bartack

### SPI — Stitches Per Inch

| Fabric Weight | Typical SPI |
|---|---|
| Light shirting | 12–14 |
| Medium apparel | 10–12 |
| Heavy bottomwear | 8–10 |
| Denim | 7–9 |

### Fabric-to-Garment Flow

1. **Raw fabric** from LD Silk Mills / Cotton suppliers
2. **Cutting room** — pattern making, cutting (cutting master)
3. **Stitching lines** — sewing per spec (stitching master + line supervisors)
4. **Finishing** — washing, pressing, final QC (finishing master)
5. **Packing + dispatch** — to buyer or Vhagar warehouse
