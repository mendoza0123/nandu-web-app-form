> Linkd is NOT a single-sided business. It runs three distinct books on the same machines. Never conflate them — each has different economics, different playbooks, different strategic value.

- **Economics: BREAK-EVEN for Linkd.** Pays the bills and absorbs fixed cost but does NOT generate meaningful margin.

### Book B1 — External Traders (~most of current 36,597 mtrs/month)
- Current external book is **mostly traders** — middlemen who get fabric printed to sell downstream.
- **Economics**: Thin margin, transactional relationship, easy to switch printers, price-sensitive.
- These are the ~75 registered customers (active subset is smaller). Verified 28 distinct parties in T12 (from Linkd jobwork ERP extract, excludes captive).
- 80% repeat rate = strong signal that the product quality retains even trader-grade customers.
- Revenue has been flat for months despite headroom — traders don't scale volume, they arbitrage margins.
- **Avg Book B jobwork rate**: ₹45.97/mtr — higher than Book A (₹36.75/mtr), confirming external work carries better margin.
- **Strategy**: Maintain as baseline volume. Do NOT invest to grow this layer. Apply price floors; let unprofitable traders go to competitors.

### Book B2 — Direct Brands (0 TODAY — THE GROWTH TARGET)
- Linkd has **zero direct-brand relationships today.**
- Target customers: apparel brands, D2C labels, fashion houses, uniform suppliers who buy printing as a direct service.
- **Economics**: Higher margin, stickier relationships, design-led conversations, larger wallet share per account.

## SECTION 11 — SCIENCE: PRINTING TECHNOLOGY

### How Sublimation Printing Works
1. Design digitized in RGB/CMYK
2. Printed onto transfer paper using sublimation dye ink
3. Heat pressed onto fabric at 180–220°C
4. Dye turns solid → gas → embeds permanently into polyester fibers
5. Result: vibrant, washfast, feel-free print that is part of the fabric (not sitting on top)

### Critical Rule: Sublimation = Polyester ONLY
| Fabric Composition | Suitability |
|---|---|
| 100% polyester | Best results, brightest colors |
| Poly-rich blends (>65% poly) | Acceptable, slightly muted |
| Poly <50% | NOT suitable |
| Natural fibers (cotton, silk, linen) | NOT suitable — design washes off |

**Never promise sublimation on natural fibers. This is a non-negotiable quality rule.**

### Paper Quality — Linkd's Moat
| Paper Type | Ink Release | Quality | Cost | Linkd Choice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Korean paper** | ~95%+ | Sharp, vibrant, consistent | Higher | **YES** |
| Chinese paper | ~80% | Inconsistent, defect-prone | Lower | No |

**The paper quality is a competitive moat.** Korean paper saves in defect rate, customer satisfaction, and repeat business — the cheap paper "saves pennies, costs crores."

### Ink at Linkd
- **Brand**: PANTONE Royal Splash Series — CMYK: Z Black, Yellow, Magenta, Cyan. Water-based dye sublimation.
- **Shelf life**: 12 months. Stored at 15–25°C.
- **Type**: Sublimation dye ink — standard for poly fabrics (Linkd's core)
- **Not used**: Reactive ink (cotton/silk — requires steaming + washing, not Linkd's scope); Pigment ink (surface-sits, less wash-fast)

### Korean Paper Specs
- **Roll size**: 1,000 m/roll
- **Width × GSM options**: 36" 38GSM | 64" 38GSM | 63" 45GSM | 72" 38GSM
- 63"/45GSM = heavier paper for wide-format premium jobs
- **Operating environment**: 21–25°C, 45–60% humidity — controlled environment is mandatory for consistent ink release and paper tension

### Color Science
- **ICC profiles**: calibrate monitor → printer → fabric for consistent color. Must be maintained.
- **Delta E**: unit of color difference. <2 = imperceptible; 2–5 = noticeable; >5 = obvious mismatch.
- **Pantone matching**: achievable with calibration. Set expectations on day one — not all Pantone colors translate perfectly.
- **Calibration cadence**: profile + ink batch checked weekly. Color drift kills repeat business silently.
- **D65 light box**: mandatory for color approval. No exceptions.

### Common Print Defects
| Defect | Cause |
|---|---|
| Banding | Visible horizontal stripes — printhead issues |
| Ghosting | Double image — fabric shift during press |
| Color drift | Hue changes across batch — ink/profile issues |
| Fading edges | Uneven heat press pressure |
| Spotting | Ink splatters or paper-defect transfers |
| Sharpness loss | Low DPI source files, paper quality, or temperature issues |

### Quality Standards (Linkd)
- Pre-production sample approval before every production run
- Color drift monitoring per run
- 100% visual inspection of output (not statistical sampling for critical jobs)
- Customer-approved reference kept on file
